- Portable locked uv launch and personal-account secure credential CLI.
- Seven workflow tools for summaries, comparisons, recovery, adherence, preview/commit and workout export.
- Raw-field activity/wellness adapters preserve zero/false values, correct weighted power, and make intensity units explicit.
